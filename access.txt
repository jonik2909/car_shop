# Image Picker Permissions and Setup

## iOS:
Add the following keys to your ios/Runner/Info.plist file:
<key>NSPhotoLibraryUsageDescription</key>
<string>Need access to your photo library to pick images.</string>
<key>NSCameraUsageDescription</key>
<string>Need access to your camera to take pictures.</string>

## Android:
Add these permissions to your android/app/src/main/AndroidManifest.xml file:
<uses-permission android:name="android.permission.CAMERA" />
<uses-permission android:name="android.permission.READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE" />
<uses-permission android:name="android.permission.WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE" />

## Then, run these commands in your terminal:
flutter clean
flutter pub get
flutter run
